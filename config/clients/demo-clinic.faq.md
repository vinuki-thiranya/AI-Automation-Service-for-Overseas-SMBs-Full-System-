# Harbourside Dental — FAQ (demo, fictional)

## Do you take new patients?
Yes. Book a "New patient exam with x-rays" (60 minutes). New-patient appointments start no later than 4 pm.

## Do you bulk bill or accept health funds?
We claim on the spot with all major health funds via HICAPS. Check-ups and cleans are gap-free with most extras policies. We don't bulk bill, except under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule for eligible children.

## How much is a check-up?
A check-up and clean is $220 and takes 45 minutes. It's gap-free with most health funds.

## Do you see children?
Yes, from age 2. Dr Sam Patel has a special interest in children and nervous patients.

## What should I do in a dental emergency?
During opening hours, message us and we'll offer the next emergency slot (from $150). After hours, call the Sydney Dental Hospital emergency line. If it's life-threatening, call 000.

## Where do I park?
Two-hour street parking is available on Example Street, and the Wilson car park is 150 m away.

## Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. Afterpay is available for treatments over $300.

## Do you offer Invisalign and whitening?
Yes. Consultations for both are free and take 30–45 minutes.

## What's your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Late cancellations may incur a $50 fee.
