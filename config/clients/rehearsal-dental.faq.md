# Bayside Family Dental — FAQ (rehearsal client, fictional)

## Do you take new patients?
Yes. Book a "New patient exam with x-rays" (60 minutes). New-patient appointments start no later than 3:30 pm.

## Do you bulk bill or accept health funds?
We claim on the spot with all major health funds via HICAPS, and check-ups and cleans have no gap with most extras cover. We're not a bulk-billing clinic, except for eligible children under the Child Dental Benefits Schedule.

## How much is a check-up?
A check-up and clean is $199 and takes 45 minutes. There's no gap with most extras cover.

## Do you see children?
Yes, from their first birthday. Dr Tom Okafor sees most of our children and anyone who feels nervous.

## What should I do in a dental emergency?
During opening hours, message us and we'll offer the next emergency slot (from $140). After hours, call 13HEALTH on 13 43 25 84 for advice. If it's life-threatening, call 000.

## Where do I park?
Free parking behind the shops, entry from Bay Lane. There are two accessible bays near the back door.

## Can I pay in instalments?
Yes. Afterpay is available for treatments over $500.

## Do you offer Invisalign and whitening?
Yes. Consultations for both are free and take 30–45 minutes.

## What's your cancellation policy?
Please give at least 24 hours' notice so we can offer the time to someone else. Late cancellations may incur a $40 fee.

## Are you open late?
Yes, on Thursdays we're open until 7 pm.
