# Northside Physio — FAQ (demo, fictional)

## Do I need a GP referral?
No. You can book directly. If you're on a GP Chronic Disease Management plan (EPC), bring the referral to your first visit.

## What should I wear?
Comfortable clothes you can move in. Shorts are handy for knee, hip and ankle problems.

## How long is the first appointment?
An initial consultation is 60 minutes: assessment, a clear explanation and your first treatment.

## Can I claim on my health fund?
Yes, with extras cover. We claim on the spot via HICAPS, so you only pay the gap.

## Do you treat work or road injuries?
Yes. Bring your WorkSafe or TAC claim number and we'll bill them directly once the claim is approved.

## Is there parking?
Free customer parking behind the building, entry via Example Lane.
