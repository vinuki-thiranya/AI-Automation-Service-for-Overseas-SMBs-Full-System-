# One-page scope and price (proposal template)

Send within 24 hours of the discovery call (spec §6.1), as a PDF or an email. One page: if it doesn't fit, the scope is too big. Run `node scripts/scope-check.ts --packages <letters> requirements.txt` on what they asked for first, and put anything it flags under "Not included" or into a change request.

Price on value, not hours (spec §7): one recovered patient a week usually pays for the whole system. Say that with their own numbers from the call, not industry statistics.
