# Staff training session (60 minutes, recorded)

Days 13–14 (spec §6.3), after go-live. Run it on a video call with the front-desk team and the owner; record it and add the recording to the dashboard's Help page (`client_resources`, kind `walkthrough_video`). Use the clinic's real dashboard and a test phone, never slides.

**Bring:** a test phone on the clinic's WhatsApp number, the dashboard signed in as the practice manager, the one-page quick guide (below) as a PDF.

| Minutes | Topic | Show, then let them try |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | What changed for patients | Message the clinic from the test phone. Point out: it says it's a virtual assistant, answers from the clinic's own information, and offers real times. |
| 5–15 | Hand-overs: the one thing staff must do | A hand-over arrives on a staff phone. Tap "I'll take it", reply, and see the assistant pause for 2 hours. What happens if nobody takes it (backup after 10 minutes). Each person tries one. |
| 15–25 | The dashboard: today's work | Overview sentence and figures; the heatmap (click an after-hours hour); open a conversation; search for "parking". Staff accounts don't see billing. |
| 25–35 | Bookings | Where bookings land in the calendar, how they're labelled, reminders, what happens when a patient replies to a reminder. What to do if a booking looks wrong (fix it in the calendar as usual; the assistant reads the calendar live). |
| 35–45 | When something looks wrong | The assistant said something odd: screenshot it and send it to the support channel. It doesn't answer at all: the support channel; we're alerted automatically within minutes. Price or hours changed: tell us, we update it (care plan hours). |
| 45–55 | Questions, and a run-through by them | Someone from the team plays a patient; someone else takes the hand-over. |
| 55–60 | Where help lives | The Help page (this recording, the quick guide, the support contact), the care plan's reply time, how to ask for changes. |

## One-page quick guide (send as a PDF after the session)

**{Clinic} front desk: how the assistant works**

- **It answers** WhatsApp, SMS and website chat, any time, from your price list, hours and policies. It always says it's a virtual assistant.
- **It books** into your calendar, only into free times, and sends reminders {24 hours / 2 hours} before.
- **It hands over** when a patient asks for a person, when it isn't sure, or when something fails. You get a card on {WhatsApp}: tap **I'll take it**, then reply as normal. It stays quiet in that conversation for 2 hours.
- **Nobody took it?** After {10} minutes, {backup person} is alerted.
- **Missed a call?** The caller gets a text within a minute offering to help by message.
- **See everything** at the dashboard: {dashboard link}. Press `?` there for shortcuts.
- **Something wrong or changed?** Message us in {support channel}. We reply within {24 / 8 / 4} hours on your care plan.
