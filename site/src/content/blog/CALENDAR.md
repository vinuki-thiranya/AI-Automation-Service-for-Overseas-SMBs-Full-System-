# Blog calendar: 12 posts answering what clinic owners search

Audience: owners and practice managers of Australian dental and physio clinics (spec §2, §9). Three posts at launch, then one a month.
Each post answers one search query plainly, with no invented statistics. It ends with one link to the relevant
package or vertical page. Posts are in `src/content/blog/<slug>.mdx` and are registered in `posts.ts`.
Only posts registered there are published; the rest of this list stays here until written.

## Outlines for posts 4 to 12

**4. Can patients book by WhatsApp?** Who in Australia uses WhatsApp vs SMS. What a WhatsApp Business number is. How booking by chat works with your calendar. The 24-hour reply window and approved templates for reminders. Set-up time. Link: AI Receptionist.

**5. Missed-call text-back.** What happens when a call rings out. The 30-second text. What the caller can do next (book, ask, request a call back). Using your existing number with call forwarding. When it's not worth it (very low call volume). Link: AI Receptionist.

**6. How fast to reply.** Why speed matters to someone choosing between clinics. What "fast" means at 9 pm vs 9 am. Measuring your own reply time. Ways to be fast without hiring. Link: Lead Qualifier.

**7. Reducing no-shows.** Reminders at 24 hours and 2 hours. Making it easy to reschedule by reply. A no-show follow-up that rebooks instead of scolding. A cancellation policy that's stated up front. Link: Physio vertical.

**8. SMS and the Spam Act.** Transactional messages (reminders) vs marketing (recalls, offers). Consent, identifying the sender and an unsubscribe option. Honouring STOP straight away. Record-keeping. *Check every legal statement with a lawyer before publishing.*

**9. Health-fund questions.** The questions patients actually ask. Writing one clear policy. Making sure every channel answers it the same way. Link: Dental vertical.

**10. Cliniko booking vs messaging.** Online booking pages suit patients who know what they want. Messaging suits questions first, then booking. Using both together. Link: Physio vertical.

**11. AI and patient privacy.** What the assistant sees and stores. Not asking about symptoms. Retention and deletion. Separating each clinic's data. Questions to ask any vendor. *Review the privacy wording before publishing.*

**12. Five Monday numbers.** Enquiries, bookings, reply time, hand-offs to staff, and estimated revenue from bookings. What each one tells you, and what to do when it moves. Link: Packages.
